Two lines for make.fun. Line 1 explains one everyday thing, a queue, to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence built on an analogy. Line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works: what it maps and what it leaves out. The entry program writes the two lines, at most 400 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479/requirements

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py`; it writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

A queue is a human train: everyone stands in a line, each person the carriage behind the one in front, all heading for the same ticket window.
The train picture works because it shows the order and the waiting, but it leaves out the way people shuffle, sigh, and change their minds about staying.
